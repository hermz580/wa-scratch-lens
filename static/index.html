How the estimates work

Any prize: uses the published overall odds. Top-prize and payout estimates: estimate remaining tickets as overall-odds denominator × all reported remaining prizes. This assumes winning and losing tickets deplete proportionally and claims track sales. It does not reveal retailer inventory or ticket location.

Non-cash prizes without a supported dollar value are excluded from payout rankings rather than counted as $0.

No result guarantees a win or profit. “Any prize” can include break-even prizes. Estimates use published prize counts and assumptions; lottery outcomes are random. Spend only what you can afford to lose.
