How the Value Rating works

Each game scores 0–100 based on:

  • Return value (45%) — how much you typically get back per dollar on unsold tickets.
  • Prize pattern (30%) — how odds have changed since launch. Better if big prizes outlast small ones.
  • Premium prize share (25%) — what % of wins come from the top 3 prizes vs. all wins.
  • Games where the big prize is gone score 45% lower and show "Skip".

Your win chance is simulated 3,000 times at your budget using real remaining odds.

Unsold tickets estimated as: overall odds × wins left. Lottery reports claimed wins, so unclaimed winners may still show as available.
